14. を
15. に/へ

17. と/や

## N3 — Intermediate
30+ units covering:
- nuanced particles;
- conditionals;
- passive/causative;
- honorific foundations;
- written grammar;
- discourse markers;
- abstract vocabulary;
- news-style reading;
- medium listening;
- opinion and explanation;
- workplace communication;
- timed JLPT practice;
- error-driven remediation.

## N2 — Upper-intermediate
30+ units covering:
- formal written Japanese;
- advanced connectors;
- modality;
- inference;
- formal emails;
- workplace meetings;
- reports;
- editorials;
- high-density reading;
- rapid listening;
- honorific refinement;
- kanji compounds;
- JLPT simulation.

## N1 — Advanced
30+ units covering:
- high-level written grammar;
- register;
- rhetoric;
- implication;
- abstract and academic vocabulary;
- editorials;
- essays;
- technical/general nonfiction;
- high-speed listening;
- business nuance;
- dense kanji compounds;
- N1 simulation and targeted remediation.

## Per-unit content contract

Every unit should have:
- objectives;
- 15–40 vocabulary items depending on level;
- 3–10 grammar targets;
- kanji references;
- 5+ example sentences;
- listening;
- reading;
- speaking/shadowing;
- quiz;
- application;
- mastery gate;
- remedial branch.

Content can be generated/curated in batches but must pass validator and human review before release.
